What is MarginMail?

MarginMail is a native Mac email app for Gmail and Google Workspace, with a calendar and a personal CRM built in. It notes what needs you (follow-ups, meeting invites and requests), helps you find a meeting time as you type, and gives everyone you write to a page. Its AI is optional: Apple Intelligence on your Mac, or your own Claude when you click. There’s no MarginMail server, and the AI never sends email for you.

FAQ

Questions

What is MarginMail?

MarginMail is a native Mac email app for Gmail and Google Workspace, with a calendar and a personal CRM built in. It notes what needs you, helps you find meeting times as you type, and gives everyone you write to a page. Its optional AI uses Apple Intelligence on your Mac or your own Claude, and never sends email.

Is there a native Gmail app for Mac?

Google doesn’t make a Gmail app for the Mac; Gmail runs in a browser. MarginMail is a native macOS app built on the Gmail API, so your Gmail and Google Workspace accounts get real Mac windows, notifications and keyboard shortcuts, with your calendar and the people you write to in the same window.

Does MarginMail store my emails on its servers?

No. There is no MarginMail server for your mail. MarginMail connects directly to Google from your Mac, so your messages stay with you and Google. MarginMail’s own small settings live in your Google Drive’s hidden app folder. There are no analytics in the app, so we can’t see how you use it.

Does MarginMail have a built-in personal CRM?

Yes. Everyone you write to gets a page built from your mail and calendar: past conversations, meetings, notes, tasks and their local time, grouped by company. Tasks close themselves when the reply or file arrives. It stays in your own Google account. Nothing is uploaded to a CRM service, and there’s nothing to import.

Is MarginMail’s AI private?

Yes. Request notes use Apple Intelligence on your Mac, so nothing leaves it. If you choose, Write it and What’s been decided use your own Claude, signed in on your Mac, only when you click. Only that one conversation goes to Anthropic, under your own plan. MarginMail never sees your Claude login, uses no API key, and runs no AI server.

Can I use MarginMail without AI?

Yes. MarginMail works fully with AI off. Follow-up and meeting notes, live words, the calendar, Find a time, People and Companies all run on plain rules. Turning AI on adds Request notes, which are found on your Mac, and Write it, which drafts replies with your own Claude.

Can MarginMail’s AI send emails for me?

No, never. MarginMail’s AI can draft a reply, but only you can send it. Before you do, MarginMail flags anything the draft added that the thread didn’t have, such as a new recipient, a link, a number or a code, and labels which AI wrote it.

Does MarginMail work with multiple Gmail accounts?

Yes. You can add several Gmail and Google Workspace accounts and see them in one unified inbox, with your calendars together beside it. MarginMail supports Google accounts today. iCloud and other providers come later.

Does MarginMail have a calendar?

Yes. Your Google Calendar sits beside your inbox. Invitations you haven’t answered look different and gather in a Needs your answer list. Press A, M or D to accept, maybe or decline, and the invite’s email files itself. Find a time shows both calendars side by side when Google shares theirs with you, and places the hold.

Can I see my email and calendar in one window on Mac?

Yes. MarginMail’s Split view shows your inbox, the message and today’s calendar side by side. Click a name to see that person’s page. Answer invites with A, M or D, and their email files itself. It works with several Google accounts and calendars at once.

How do follow-up reminders work in MarginMail?

When you’re waiting on a reply, MarginMail notices and writes a note in the reply bar, like “You asked Ava on Oct 9. No reply in 6 days.” The note joins your Margins stack so you can nudge, mark it Done or send it to the back of the stack. It works on rules, with AI off.

Does MarginMail use read receipts or tracking pixels?

No. MarginMail never adds read receipts or open tracking to the mail you send. It also blocks remote images in mail you receive by default, so senders’ tracking pixels don’t load unless you allow images for that sender.

How much does MarginMail cost?

MarginMail is free during the beta. Pricing will be announced before the beta ends, well before anything changes for you. Joining the beta only needs your email address, and the only email we’ll send is your invite.

Is there a MarginMail app for iPhone?

Not yet. MarginMail starts on the Mac, where your mail, calendar and people share one window. An iPhone companion app is planned for later. Until then, your mail stays in Gmail as usual, so you can keep using Gmail on your phone.

Is MarginMail the same as the Margin app for iPhone?

No. MarginMail is our own Apple-native email app for the Mac, built for Gmail and Google Workspace, with an iPhone companion app planned for later. It isn’t affiliated with other apps named Margin. Everything about MarginMail is on marginmail.ai, and the beta is free to join.

What Mac do I need for MarginMail?

MarginMail needs macOS 26 or later. Request notes use Apple Intelligence, so they need a Mac where Apple Intelligence is available and turned on. Everything else, including the calendar, follow-up notes, live words and People, works without it.
