Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

MarginMail is a mail and calendar app for the Mac, in private testing. An iPhone companion app comes later. It works with Google accounts (Gmail and Google Workspace). There’s no MarginMail server for your mail: it goes directly between your Mac and Google.

The Google data MarginMail uses, and why

When you sign in with Google, MarginMail asks for the access below. Each kind is used only for the features listed, on your own Mac.

  • Gmail (read, organize, draft and send): to download and show your mail, apply the actions you choose (Done, labels, read and unread, spam, Trash), save drafts and send mail you write. MarginMail never permanently deletes mail.
  • Gmail settings (filters and send-as addresses): to add or remove a Gmail filter when you use Block sender, and to change an alias’s name, reply-to address or signature when you edit it in MarginMail.
  • Google Calendar: to show and edit your events, add Google Meet links, answer invitations, place holds, choose which calendars show and their colors, and show guests’ busy times when Google shares them with you. MarginMail never deletes calendars or changes how they’re shared.
  • Google Contacts and “Other contacts” (read-only): for recipient suggestions and contact photos. MarginMail never changes your contacts.
  • Google Drive, limited to files MarginMail creates and its own hidden app folder: to share large attachments as Drive links, and to keep MarginMail’s settings, notes, snoozes and follow-ups so your devices stay in step. MarginMail can’t see your other Drive files.
  • Your name, email address and photo: to show which account is signed in.

Where your data lives

  • On your Mac. MarginMail keeps a copy of your mail, calendar and contacts in its own folder so it’s fast and works offline. Your Google sign-in is kept in the macOS Keychain on each device and is never synced. Every connection to Google is encrypted in transit.
  • In your own Google account. Your mail, drafts, calendar and contacts stay with Google as they always have. MarginMail’s own settings, notes, snoozes and follow-ups are stored in your Google account’s hidden Drive app folder.
  • Nowhere else. There’s no MarginMail server for your data. MarginMail’s developer can’t see your mail, calendar, contacts or MarginMail’s own settings and notes, and keeps no copy of them.

No analytics or tracking in the app

MarginMail contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. We cannot see how you use the app. MarginMail blocks tracking pixels in the email you receive and never adds read receipts or open tracking to the email you send.

AI features

AI features are optional and off until you turn them on. MarginMail works fully without them.

  • Apple Intelligence runs on your Mac. What it reads stays on your Mac.
  • Your own Claude. If you install Anthropic’s Claude Code, sign in with your own Anthropic account and choose Connect Claude in Settings › Intelligence, MarginMail starts Claude on your Mac when you click Write it or What’s been decided. Claude reads that one conversation and your note. That content goes to Anthropic under your own account, and Anthropic’s terms and your Claude settings decide how long Anthropic keeps it and whether it’s used to improve their models. MarginMail’s developer never receives it.
  • AI can suggest a draft or a label. It can’t send, permanently delete or create filters, and it never gets your Google sign-in or contacts Google directly. MarginMail treats email content as untrusted and doesn’t follow instructions found inside a message.
  • MarginMail doesn’t use your Google data to develop, improve or train any AI or machine-learning model.
  • Disconnect Claude in Settings › Intelligence and nothing more is sent.

How we treat your Google data

MarginMail’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

  • Your Google data is used only for the features described on this page.
  • It isn’t sold, used for advertising, given to data brokers or other third parties, or used to decide credit or lending.
  • The only transfer is the one you choose: a conversation sent to your own Claude.
  • Nobody at MarginMail reads it, because we have no access to it. If you send us a screenshot or a message for support, we see only what you send.

Keeping and deleting your data

MarginMail keeps its copy on your Mac only while an account is connected. Remove account (Settings › Accounts) deletes that account’s mail, calendar and contact data, drafts and caches from your Mac, and your mail is left as it is with Google.

MarginMail’s settings, notes, snoozes and follow-ups stay in your Google account until you delete them: open Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › MarginMail › Delete hidden app data. You can revoke MarginMail’s access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

We hold no copy, so there’s nothing to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If MarginMail starts using Google data in a new way, we’ll update this page and ask for your OK in the app before the change applies.

Beta waitlist

If you join the beta waitlist on marginmail.ai, we store your email address, the date you joined, your place in line, your referral code, the referral code that brought you (if any), and the campaign name in the link you used (such as a utm_source tag), if any. We use it only to email you when your beta invite is ready. We won’t send newsletters or other email unless you ask for them. The waitlist runs on our own Google Cloud project; it sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party list service. To limit abuse we keep a salted, one-way hash of your IP address for about two hours; we never store the address itself. When we send invites we may use an email delivery provider (such as SendGrid) to send them on our behalf. To be removed from the waitlist, write to hello@marginmail.ai and we will delete your entry.

Site visit counts

This website (marginmail.ai) counts visits anonymously on our own server so we can see which pages are useful. It sets no cookies, uses no third-party analytics or ads, and doesn’t identify, fingerprint or follow you. When a page loads, your browser sends one small message with: the page’s address, the website that linked you here (only its domain, such as news.ycombinator.com, or “direct”), any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source or a referral code), and a size class for your screen (phone, tablet or desktop). Our server adds a country when our hosting provides one. It then adds 1 to that day’s totals and keeps nothing else: no IP address, no user ID and no browsing history. When you join the waitlist we also add 1 to that day’s signup totals for the page and campaign you joined from. If you watch the video tour, your browser sends the same kind of small message when the player opens, when it starts playing, at each quarter and at the end (and when the animated tour on the home page finishes); each adds 1 to that day’s total for that step, with the page, its version and your screen’s size class, and nothing that tells one viewer from another. When you download the beta through our download link, we count that the same way: our server adds 1 to that day’s download total, with the link’s campaign tag (if any) and whether the browser is on a phone, tablet or computer, then sends you on to the file. Abuse protection uses the same short-lived, salted hash described above, deleted within about two hours. We keep the daily totals. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.
