Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

MarginMail is a mail and calendar app for the Mac, in private testing. An iPhone companion app comes later. There’s no MarginMail server for your mail: it goes between your Mac and Google.

What MarginMail accesses

When you sign in with Google, MarginMail asks for access to your Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, and files in Google Drive that MarginMail creates or that you open with it. MarginMail uses this access only to show, organize, send and sync your own mail, calendar and contacts at your request.

Where your data lives

  • Your mail, calendar and contacts are processed on your own devices.
  • MarginMail's settings and notes sync through your own Google account (Drive app data) and, in future versions, your own iCloud account.
  • MarginMail does not operate servers that receive, store or process your email. We do not retain your data.

No analytics or tracking in the app

MarginMail contains no analytics, advertising or tracking. We cannot see how you use the app. MarginMail blocks tracking pixels in the email you receive and never adds read receipts or open tracking to the email you send.

AI features

AI features are optional and off unless you turn them on. If you connect an AI provider you already use (for example, your own Claude account), email content you choose is sent only to that provider, under your agreement with them. MarginMail never sells or shares your data, and does not use it to train models.

Google API Services

MarginMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not read by humans except with your explicit permission, for security purposes, or to comply with law.

Deleting your data

Signing out of MarginMail removes its local data from your device. You can revoke MarginMail's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which also lets you delete the app data MarginMail stored in your Google Drive.

Beta waitlist

If you join the beta waitlist on marginmail.ai, we store your email address, the date you joined, your place in line, your referral code, the referral code that brought you (if any), and the campaign name in the link you used (such as a utm_source tag), if any. We use it only to email you when your beta invite is ready. We won’t send newsletters or other email unless you ask for them. The waitlist runs on our own Google Cloud project; it sets no cookies and uses no analytics or third-party list service. To limit abuse we keep a salted, one-way hash of your IP address for about two hours; we never store the address itself. When we send invites we may use an email delivery provider (such as SendGrid) to send them on our behalf. To be removed from the waitlist, write to hello@marginmail.ai and we will delete your entry.

Site visit counts

This website (marginmail.ai) counts visits anonymously on our own server so we can see which pages are useful. It sets no cookies, uses no third-party analytics or ads, and doesn’t identify, fingerprint or follow you. When a page loads, your browser sends one small message with: the page’s address, the website that linked you here (only its domain, such as news.ycombinator.com, or “direct”), any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source or a referral code), and a size class for your screen (phone, tablet or desktop). Our server adds a country when our hosting provides one. It then adds 1 to that day’s totals and keeps nothing else: no IP address, no user ID and no browsing history. When you join the waitlist we also add 1 to that day’s signup totals for the page and campaign you joined from. If you watch the video tour, your browser sends the same kind of small message when the player opens, when it starts playing, at each quarter and at the end (and when the animated tour on the home page finishes); each adds 1 to that day’s total for that step, with the page, its version and your screen’s size class, and nothing that tells one viewer from another. Abuse protection uses the same short-lived, salted hash described above, deleted within about two hours. We keep the daily totals. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, nothing is counted.
