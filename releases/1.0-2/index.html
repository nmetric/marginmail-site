MarginMail 1.0 (2)

Mon, Nov 2 · Beta

The first MarginMail beta is a native Mac app for Gmail and Google Workspace, with your calendar and a page for everyone you write to in the same window. Start with Help › Take the Quick Tour, then press ⇧⌘M for today’s notes and type Thursday in a reply to see your free times. MarginMail’s AI is optional and can draft a reply, and only you can send it. MarginMail updates itself when you restart or quit, and Help › Send Feedback reaches us anytime.

Made with love for the Mac.
