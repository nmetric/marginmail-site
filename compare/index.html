Compare MarginMail with other mail apps

Short answer: MarginMail is a native Mac app for Gmail and Google Workspace that puts your mail, calendar and people in one window, notes what needs you, and runs optional AI on your Mac or through your own Claude, with no MarginMail server. Every mail app makes different trade-offs. These pages say plainly where each one is the better choice.

Facts checked October 16, 2026. We re-check each page at least every quarter and whenever a product changes its pricing. If something is out of date, write to hello@marginmail.ai.

MarginMail is the better choice when

  • You use Gmail or Google Workspace (one account or several) on a Mac.
  • You want your calendar and the people you write to in the same window as your mail.
  • You want AI that’s optional, runs on your Mac or your own Claude, and never sends.
  • You’d rather no vendor server sat between you and Google.

MarginMail isn’t the right choice (yet) when

  • You need iCloud, Microsoft 365 or IMAP accounts.
  • You need Windows, Android or a full iPhone app today.
  • Your team shares an inbox.
  • You rely on read receipts or open tracking (MarginMail never offers them).
